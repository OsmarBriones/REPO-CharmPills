# Changelog

## [Unreleased]

### Added
- Numbness: while the Pills are in the pocket, incoming damage is multiplied by `DamageMultiplier` (default 0.5, never below 1 point) and the valuable wears 12% per softened hit.
- Settings `DamageMultiplier` (0.1 to 1) and `MinimumHit` (1 to 100).
- `Debug.DebugMode` (off by default): puts the Pills on the floor at the start of each level and writes detailed logs.
